Heartbeats is end-to-end encrypted accounting infrastructure for agents. Bring your own agents, or run our reference ones. The system is temporal, append-only, and attribution-enforced, giving every action a traceable history with full time travel. The open source client SDK owns the encryption boundary. Accounting runs on a bitemporal ledger (system time and world time).
